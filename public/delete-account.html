GlucoLab Pro Privacy Policy
Official Data & Account Deletion Portal

Delete Account & Health Records

GlucoLab respects your privacy and personal data ownership. Request permanent deletion of your account, blood sugar entries, insulin dosing logs, and notification tokens.

Data That Will Be Permanently Erased

  • Blood Glucose Readings: All historical measurements, tags, and symptoms.
  • Insulin Dosages: Basal and bolus entries and schedules.
  • Weight & Lab Records: Weight logs and HbA1c test data.
  • Device Tokens: Firebase push tokens and OneSignal subscription IDs.
  • Account Information: Name, registered email address, and authentication credentials.

Retention Policy & Zero Residual Data

Immediate Purge: When confirmed, all database rows and authentication entries are permanently purged.

No Secondary Retention: GlucoLab does not retain residual copies of your medical logs or personal information for advertising, analytics, or resale.

Tip: Export your doctor-ready reports to PDF or Excel from the Reports tab in the app before initiating deletion.

Submit Account Deletion Request

Warning: Deletion is irreversible. Once processed, your historical glucose logs and account cannot be recovered.

How to Delete Directly in the Mobile App

  1. Open the GlucoLab Pro app on your Android or iOS device.
  2. Tap the Settings tab in the bottom menu.
  3. Scroll to the Account & Security section.
  4. Select Delete Account & Medical Data.
  5. Confirm your choice to immediately purge all local and cloud records.

Contact Data Protection Team

If you experience any difficulties or require data export assistance, contact our privacy compliance officer directly at support@glucolab.app.
